How can I use Canvas on my mobile device?

The Canvas app is the best way to use Canvas on your phone or tablet. It is free to download, and you log in with your Canvas account.

Download the app

  • Android (Google Play)
  • iPhone (App Store)

Video walkthrough

What you can do in the app

  • See your courses and groups
  • View and submit assignments, take quizzes, and join discussions
  • Check your grades
  • Use the calendar and To Do list
  • Read notifications and Inbox messages

Good to know

  • Not every Canvas feature is in the app. Some pages, such as Conferences and Collaborations, do not work on mobile.
  • Some actions, such as certain quizzes, open your phone's browser from inside the app.
  • You can also open Canvas in a phone browser, but some features may not work as expected. If something does not work, try a computer.
